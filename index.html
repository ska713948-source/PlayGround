Our philosophy

Fewer, better things.

We make a small number of pieces each season, slowly, with mills and makers we know by name. Every seam is considered. Every fibre is traceable. Nothing is designed to be replaced next year.

Our clothes are meant to be lived in — softened by wear, mended when needed, and kept for a very long time.
